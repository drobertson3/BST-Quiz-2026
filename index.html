🎯 Exactly right — 30 XP. You gave the same mark as the teacher.

        
👏 One mark out — 15 XP.

        
🤏 Two marks out — 5 XP.

        
Answers are anonymous, and you'll never be given your own work.

Everything is scheduled per term, so the deck focuses itself on what you haven't learnt yet.
